# Lodging: properties, calendar and bookings (T-160)

Hotel businesses sell places to stay: whole villas and per-room homestays. Rapiz is the only source of truth for their calendar: there is no sync with Google Sheets, iCal or OTAs. The backend module is `lodging` ([MODULE.md](../apps/backend/src/modules/lodging/MODULE.md)). The decision records are [ADR-018](adr/ADR-018-lodging-module.md) (module) and [ADR-019](adr/ADR-019-bot-lodging-holds.md) (bot holds); the designs are the [part 1 spec](superpowers/specs/2026-11-01-lodging-calendar-design.md) and the [part 3 spec](superpowers/specs/2026-11-01-bot-lodging-holds-design.md). Units with their own details and bedroom packages (T-193): [ADR-027](adr/ADR-027-lodging-units-and-packages.md), [spec](superpowers/specs/2026-11-05-lodging-units-and-packages-design.md).

Screens (hotel businesses only; the sidebar shows **Lịch · Booking · Căn hộ** instead of products):

| Screen | Route | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lịch | `/app/calendar` | Nightly grid of every property and room, "Tìm trống" search, create holds, bookings and blocks |
| Lịch căn | `/app/calendar/$propertyId` (`?view=month\|quarter\|year\|list&from=&unit=`) | The calendar of one property: a month wall calendar (default), three months, a year or its booking list, with a side panel for the shown range |
| Booking | `/app/bookings` | Lists: Hôm nay, Giữ chỗ (with the count of bot holds waiting for staff), Sắp tới, Tất cả; filters by place, tag, source and status; the booking sheet with status actions, edits and history |
| Căn hộ | `/app/properties`, `/app/properties/$propertyId` | Property cards and the editor (Thông tin, Đơn vị cho thuê, Giá, Chính sách, Ảnh & video); `?tab=rooms` opens the unit list |
| Đơn vị cho thuê | `/app/properties/$propertyId/units/$unitId` | One unit of a rooms property (lazy route): tabs Thông tin, Giá, Ảnh & video (`?folder=` like the property page) |
| Căn hộ & phòng (bot training) | `/app/bots/$botId/products` | Which properties the bot sells |

Vocabulary: the Vietnamese UI calls a property **Căn hộ** (sidebar, page title, "Thêm căn hộ", "Tất cả căn hộ", bot training "Căn hộ & phòng"); **Nguyên căn** stays the name of the whole-property sell mode. English says "Properties".

The Căn hộ page has two views, remembered per user in this browser (`localStorage` key `rapiz.lodging.propertiesView.<userId>`; unavailable storage falls back to cards): **Thẻ** (card grid) and **Danh sách** (a compact table: photo, code, name, area, sell mode, standard guests, T2–T5 / T6 / T7 prices from the weekday rates, today's state, and a warning icon when the bot cannot offer the place). The table scrolls horizontally inside its container on phones.

`/app/accommodations` and `/app/stays` (T-155) redirect to `/app/properties` and `/app/bookings`. For a hotel business, `/app/products` also redirects to `/app/properties`.

## Operator workflow

1. **Set up a property** (Căn hộ → new). Give a short code (e.g. `A17`), name, area and address, and choose the sell mode: **Nguyên căn** (whole) or **Theo phòng** (rooms). Enter the standard party (adults, children), an optional hard maximum, and the child age bands: children younger than `childFreeBelow` are free within the standard children; from `childAdultFrom` a child counts as an adult. Amenities, the descriptive room layout, policies (empty = unknown, never "none") and the photos and videos (tab **Ảnh & video**, see "Photos and videos" below) are optional. A whole property gets its single whole unit automatically.
2. **Units** (rooms mode only, tab **Đơn vị cho thuê**): every room, bungalow or house is a card (cover, name, type, view, "Tiêu chuẩn N · Tối đa M", "từ" = the lowest weekday sell of its full package or plain rates). **Thêm đơn vị** asks for the name, type and standard party and opens the new unit's page. The card's switch turns the unit on or off and its ⋮ menu moves it earlier or later or deletes it; each of these saves at once (`PUT units` with every unit and all its fields). The unit page has three tabs: **Thông tin** (name, type `room | bungalow | house | other`, view, area m², beds, standard adults and children, an optional maximum, description, amenities one per line, on sale), **Giá** (that unit's packages, weekday prices and date-range prices; saving sends the whole rates request, other targets as loaded, and is blocked while another target misses prices) and **Ảnh & video** (the same folders as the property, on the unit's own gallery, see "Photos and videos"). Each unit is entered by hand; there are no room types with a quantity. A unit that has bookings cannot be deleted; deactivate it instead. A room cannot be added or reactivated while a whole-property booking holds a later night (409 with `details.codes`): that booking took the rooms of its day, so move or cancel it first. The sell mode can change only while the property has no booking ending after today.
3. **Prices** (tab Giá): weekday prices must cover all seven weekdays exactly once for every target: the whole property, or each active room of a rooms property. A rooms property may also have whole-property prices; without them a whole rental costs the sum of its rooms. Add date-range prices ("ngày đặc biệt": Tết, holidays, peak) with an optional minimum stay; ranges of one target never overlap, and a range price wins over the weekday price. Surcharges per extra adult and extra child are per person per night. Every price has a sell and a cost price.
   - **Bedroom packages** (Gói phòng, T-193): a target that is always rented to one party but opens only some bedrooms (villa "Aura 6PN" sold as 6PN, 5PN or 4PN) gets up to 10 packages, each with a name (≤ 50, unique per target ignoring case), a bedroom count (distinct per target), a standard party and an optional maximum. The section sits above the matrix on the property Giá tab (whole place, whole mode) and on the unit page (one unit). The matrix then shows a sell/cost row pair per package, most bedrooms first. The **full package** (most bedrooms; a tie goes to the later one) must price all seven weekdays; any other package prices each weekday at most once, and an empty cell ("Không bán") means that package is not sold that weekday. Every rate of a target with packages names one of them, date-range prices included (the dialog has a **Gói** select, default the full package); ranges never overlap per target and package, and a package's range price may cover a weekday that package does not sell. Packages never change occupancy: a booking takes the unit's nights whatever its package, so the closed bedrooms are never sold to someone else.
   - Adding the first packages to a target moves its weekday prices onto the new package in the editor, and on save the server moves every stored date-range price of that target without a package onto its full package (an overlap with a range price of the full package is refused). Removing the last package gives the full package's prices back to the plain rows. **Deleting a package deletes its date-range prices** (the editor asks first); bookings that used it keep their total and lose the package name.
   - A package keeps its target: moving one to another unit is a delete and a create. Whole-mode properties have no room packages (a save drops them like room prices). An inactive room keeps its packages; a new package on an inactive room is refused.
4. **Calendar** (Lịch):
   - **Find availability**: enter check-in, check-out, adults and child ages. Properties that fit are highlighted with their total and a **Giữ chỗ** button; full ones are dimmed. A rooms property offers the whole place and, when it differs, a set of free rooms. An option on a single package target names the package picked for the party ("Gói 4PN") and lists the others with their totals ("Gói khác: 3PN không bán, 6PN 30.000.000 ₫").
   - **Packages on a booking**: the create and edit forms show a **Gói** select per target with packages (default: the quote's pick); the quote names it ("Gói: 5PN"), the bar label is "guest · code · 5PN", the quick view and the booking sheet show it ("Nguyên căn · 5PN"). Blocks have no package.
   - **Hold**: select free nights (click, drag or shift-click) and save as **Giữ chỗ**. A hold takes the nights and expires after the business's hold time (default 120 minutes, setting 15–1440). Expiry is lazy: the next lodging read or write of the business marks it `expired` and frees the nights. Staff can extend a hold.
   - **Confirm** (Đặt luôn, or Xác nhận on a hold): the stay is booked.
   - **Check in** from the check-in date (Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh), **check out** afterwards. **Không đến** (no-show) and **Huỷ** (cancel, with a reason) free the nights.
   - **Early check-out**: checking out before the booked check-out moves it to the later of check-in + 1 night and today; the later nights go back to the calendar. An automatic price is re-quoted for the shorter stay (kept as it was when a night has no price); a manual price is kept. The history records the old and new check-out.
   - A hold that expired before a status action answers 409 with `details.reason = 'HOLD_EXPIRED'` ("Giữ chỗ đã hết hạn").
   - **Block** (Khoá lịch): takes nights without a guest or money, e.g. repairs or owner use. Removing the block frees them.
   - **Night notes** (tab **Ghi chú** of the create sheet): a staff-only text (≤ 300 characters) on each selected night of the property, or of each selected room. The same text is saved on every night of the range; an empty text (**Xoá ghi chú**) deletes it. A cell with a note shows a small dot, its accessible name ends with the note, and hovering or focusing it shows the note.
   - **Lịch căn** (one property's calendar): the property code and name in a Lịch row (or a phone month card's header) open `/app/calendar/$propertyId`; the chevron of a rooms property still expands its rooms. The URL holds the view (`view`: `month` default, `quarter`, `year`, `list`), the anchor month (`from`, any night of it; default today's month) and, for a rooms property, one room (`unit`; default the whole place), so links and Back restore the view. Header: ← Lịch, code, name, capacity and the internal note, ◀ Hôm nay ▶ (one month, three months or twelve months per step) and the switch **Tháng · 3 tháng · Năm · Danh sách**.
     - **Tháng**: a Monday-first month with the adjacent months' days greyed; each day shows its number (today ringed), price, date-range label and night-note dot; bookings and blocks are bars split per week with the grid's status look and hold expiry. Free nights select like the grid (click, drag, shift-click) and open the create sheet; a booked day opens the booking (its tooltip offers "Sửa ghi chú"). View-only members cannot select.
     - **3 tháng** and **Năm** (twelve months from the anchor month): compact months coloured by state (trống, giữ chỗ, đã đặt, đang ở, khoá; for a whole rooms place: trống, còn phòng, kín) with each month's occupancy; a month title (or, in Năm, the whole month) opens its Tháng view; in 3 tháng a booked day opens its booking and a free day opens its month.
     - **Danh sách**: the property's bookings (`GET /lodging/bookings?propertyId=`): Sắp tới (`view=upcoming`), Đã qua (`view=all` pages, keeping stays that checked out before today) or Tất cả, and a status; each line has the dates and nights, guest, phone (`tel:`), packages, total, status, source (**Bot tạo** for the bot) and **Mở hội thoại** when the stay has a conversation.
     - Rooms properties get a chip row **Cả căn · rooms**: Cả căn shows "x/y trống" per day (no bars) and selects whole-place stays on fully free nights; a room shows that room exactly like a whole property.
     - Side panel (right on wide screens, below otherwise; not on Danh sách) for the shown months: occupancy (taken unit-nights incl. holds and blocks / all unit-nights), nights sold and **Doanh thu booking** (the totals of the confirmed, in-house or checked-out stays with a night in the range, each booking once), the next five check-ins and check-outs in the range from today, and the bot holds waiting for staff.
   - **Internal property note** (Căn hộ → Thông tin, "Ghi chú nội bộ — bot không thấy", ≤ 2000 characters): shown in a card on the property name in the calendar's first column (hover, focus or click the note icon). It never reaches the bot.
   - **Quick view**: hovering or focusing a booking bar's cells shows the guest, phone, party, total, hold expiry, tags and the start of the booking note (blocks: the reason). A bar with a note shows a note icon; a bar with tags shows a tag icon with their count.
   - **Tags** (Nhãn): free-text labels on a stay (at most 8, each ≤ 30 characters; suggestions "Đã cọc", "Khách quen", "Cần xe đón", "Có trẻ nhỏ", "Đoàn công ty"). Set them when creating or editing a stay (checked-in stays too); they show as chips in the sheet and the bookings table, and the bookings list filters by one tag (clicking a chip filters too). Tags are labels only: no money logic, and changing them never re-prices the stay.
5. **Edit** a hold or confirmed stay: dates, rooms, party, package, guest, adjustment, note. Only a change of dates, rooms, party or package re-quotes the stay at today's prices; the nights are re-taken in one transaction when dates or rooms change, and a conflict keeps the old nights. Any other edit (guest, phone, note, tags, adjustment) keeps the stored quote and nights: the total is the stored price (manual or quoted) plus the new adjustment, so later price changes never move an agreed total. A stay saved with a **manual price** must get a new price (or explicitly go back to the automatic price) when its dates, rooms, party or package change. A package staff did not touch is kept on an edit of dates or party, unless it no longer prices every night or holds the party: then the package is re-picked automatically (no warning). The form's preview asks for the same rule (`keepPackageIds`) and shows the package the save will store. Changing the rooms re-picks automatically. A checked-in stay cannot change its package. A checked-in stay can move its check-out, correct its price (manual price, adjustment and reason) and change its tags; a manual-priced stay that moves its check-out must again choose its price. Bookings of a paused property stay editable; new bookings are refused.
6. Every save sends the version it was edited against; a stale version answers 409 and the screen reloads.

The quote is computed in code: per night the date-range price or else the weekday price, plus surcharges above the standard party, plus a signed adjustment. Warnings: `min_nights`, `missing_rate` (staff enters the price), `over_standard`, `over_max` (staff may still save), `child_ages_missing`, `package_unavailable`.

**Packages in the quote.** Each priced target of the selection that has packages gets one: the one sent in `packageIds`, else an automatic pick. The automatic pick looks at the party only for a **single package target**, i.e. when the selection prices exactly one target and that target has packages: then it takes the package with the fewest bedrooms whose maximum (or, without one, its standard party) holds adults + children and that prices every night, else the full package. In every other case (several targets with packages, or one with packages next to one without) each target gets its full package. An explicit package that does not price a night makes that night `missing_rate` (a 0 line) and adds `package_unavailable`, so staff enter a manual price. The standard party is the package's for a whole rental with a whole-target package; for a room selection it is the sum per room of its package's standard, else the room's capacity. `over_max` checks the property's maximum always, plus the package's maximum for a whole rental with a package, else the sum per room of its package's (or the room's) maximum when every one is set. The quote lists the packages it used (`packages: {target, packageId, name}[]`). A stay lasts 1–60 nights; the calendar shows up to 62 nights.

## API

All routes are authenticated, workspace-scoped and answer `404 NOT_FOUND` outside a hotel business. Contracts: `lodgingV2Routes` in `packages/contracts/src/lodging/lodging.routes.ts`. Errors: `400 VALIDATION_FAILED`, `404 NOT_FOUND`, `409 CONFLICT`; a taken night answers 409 with `details.reason = 'NIGHTS_TAKEN'` and `details.codes` (the occupying booking codes), a stale version 409 with `details.reason = 'STALE_VERSION'`, an action on a hold that has expired 409 with `details.reason = 'HOLD_EXPIRED'`. A stay without a price for every night answers 400 with `details.field = 'manualTotalSell'`.

| Route | Capability | Behaviour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GET `/lodging/properties` | `orders.view` | Properties with units, today's state (free, partial, hold, occupied, blocked) and `weekdaySell` (whole-property sell per weekday, Monday first, from the weekday rates; a rooms property without a whole rate sums its active rooms; null = not fully priced) |
| POST `/lodging/properties` | `catalog.manage` | Create (201); added to every bot that has an explicit property list |
| GET `/lodging/properties/{id}` | `orders.view` | Detail with units (with their details and galleries), packages, rates (`packageId`) and surcharges |
| PATCH `/lodging/properties/{id}` | `catalog.manage` | Partial update `{version, patch}`; sell-mode change refused while a booking ends after today |
| POST `/lodging/media/videos` | `catalog.manage` | Upload one MP4 (multipart field `file`, ≤ 25 MiB, ftyp-checked) → `{url: '/media/lodging/<ws>/<id>.mp4', bytes}`; 413 `VIDEO_TOO_LARGE`, 400 `INVALID_VIDEO`, 503 `MEDIA_STORAGE_UNAVAILABLE` |
| PUT `/lodging/properties/{id}/units` | `catalog.manage` | Replace the unit list with every field (category, view, area, description, amenities, max, gallery and cover; omitted fields take their defaults, so send each unit whole); adding or reactivating a room is refused (409) while a future whole booking exists; each unit gallery has the property gallery's limits and media checks (`field = 'units[u].gallery[f].items[i].url'`) |
| PUT `/lodging/properties/{id}/rates` | `catalog.manage` | Replace the packages, weekday rates and surcharges together, validated as a whole: `packages[]` with a client `key` (and `id` for a kept package; a missing package is deleted with its date-range prices), weekday rates name theirs by `packageKey`. 400 on a repeated key or id, a foreign or retargeted package id, a bad target, a new package on an inactive room, equal bedrooms or names, > 10 per target, or incomplete coverage; stored package-less date-range prices of a target that gets packages move onto its full package |
| POST `/lodging/properties/{id}/overrides` | `catalog.manage` | Add a date-range price (201); `packageId` is required on a target with packages and refused on one without |
| PATCH / DELETE `/lodging/properties/{id}/overrides/{rateId}` | `catalog.manage` | Edit / remove a date-range price (`?version=` on delete) |
| GET `/lodging/calendar?from&to&propertyIds` | `orders.view` | Rows per bookable unit with nightly prices and booking segments (with quick-view details: phone, party, total, tags, note), the property's `internalNote` and its night `notes` `{id, unitId, night, text}` in the window, 1–62 nights, or 1–371 when `propertyIds` names exactly one property (Lịch căn's quarter and year views) |
| PUT `/lodging/properties/{id}/notes` | `orders.manage` | Upsert the staff-only note of one night `{unitId (null = the property, else one of its rooms), night, text ≤ 300}`; an empty text deletes it; answers `{note}` (null when deleted) |
| POST `/lodging/availability` | `orders.view` | Free whole properties and room sets for the nights, with `fits` and quotes; fitting first, then cheapest; each option has `packages` and, on a single package target, `alternatives {packageId, name, bedrooms, totalSell \| null, fits}[]` (fewest bedrooms first) |
| POST `/lodging/quote` | `orders.view` | Quote of one selection (with optional `packageIds`) plus `takenBy` codes; writes nothing. Without `packageIds`, optional `keepPackageIds` (a booking edit's preview) keeps those packages while they price every night and hold the party, else picks automatically, as the PATCH does |
| GET `/lodging/bookings?view&propertyId&status&q&tag&source&conversationId&offset` | `orders.view` | `today` / `holds` / `upcoming` (default) / `all`, 100 per page; `tag` keeps bookings carrying that exact tag; `source` keeps one source (e.g. `bot`) |
| GET `/lodging/bookings/review-count` | `orders.view` | `{pending}`: bot holds waiting for staff (`source = bot`, status `hold`, `reviewedAt` null) |
| POST `/lodging/bookings` | `orders.manage` | Create a hold, confirmed stay or block (201); idempotent by `clientRequestId`; stays accept `tags` (≤ 8, each ≤ 30 chars; trimmed, duplicates dropped) and `packageIds` (`[]` = automatic); a block ignores `packageIds` |
| GET `/lodging/bookings/{id}` | `orders.view` | Detail with history; `reviewedAt` is the first staff action on a bot booking |
| PATCH `/lodging/bookings/{id}` | `orders.manage` | Edit; re-quotes only when dates, units, party or packages change, re-takes the nights atomically when dates or units change. `packageIds` omitted = keep the stored packages (re-picked automatically when the units change, or when a kept package no longer prices the nights or holds the party), `[]` = automatic, otherwise explicit |
| POST `/lodging/bookings/{id}/status` | `orders.manage` | `confirm`, `check_in`, `check_out`, `cancel`, `no_show`, `extend_hold`, `remove_block` |

| GET / PUT `/lodging/bots/{botId}/properties` | `orders.view` / `bots.manage` | The properties a bot sells (`[]` = every property) |

**Pictures.** Photos uploaded to Rapiz storage (`POST /products/images`, shared with commerce) are stored and returned as host-relative paths `/media/products/<workspaceId>/<file>.jpg|png`, so the browser loads them from whatever host serves the app and a new public host (dev tunnel restart, domain move) never breaks them. Rows written before this rule hold the absolute URL of the public host of that time; every read (web DTOs and the bot) normalises a Rapiz URL on any host to its path, and a write stores the path. When the bot sends a photo (Messenger, Zalo, website widget), the path is turned into an absolute URL on the **current** `S3_PUBLIC_URL` (`https://<public host>/media`), so that variable must point at the live public host. Only the exact key shapes Rapiz generates count as its media (uuid workspace and file names: `products/<ws>/<id>.jpg|png`, `workspaces/<ws>/bots/<bot>/<id>.jpg|png|webp`, `webchat/<ws>/<connection>/<id>.jpg|png|webp|gif`, `lodging/<ws>/<id>.mp4`); a look-alike external link such as `https://cdn.example.vn/media/products/villa-1.jpg` is kept as it is. A gallery photo that is Rapiz media must be this business's product picture (`/media/products/<workspaceId>/…`), else 400 `VALIDATION_FAILED` with `details = {reason: 'IMAGE_NOT_UPLOADED', field: 'gallery[f].items[i].url'}`; external https links stay allowed. An uploaded video must be this business's (`/media/lodging/<workspaceId>/…`), else 400 `VIDEO_NOT_UPLOADED`; links are never Rapiz media. Helpers: `apps/backend/src/common/storage/media-url.ts`.

**Photos and videos (T-192, [ADR-026](adr/ADR-026-lodging-media-folders.md)).** The **Ảnh & video** tab keeps a property's media in folders, like Google Drive: up to 20 folders (unique names, case-insensitive), 200 items in all, each a photo (uploaded through `POST /products/images` and stored as JPEG/PNG; a picked WebP is converted to JPEG), an MP4 video uploaded through `POST /lodging/media/videos` (≤ 25 MiB; the API buffers the whole file in memory before storing it) or an https video link (YouTube, Drive, TikTok…) with an optional title (a link pasted without `https://`, e.g. `youtu.be/…`, gets it and `http://` is upgraded; the API still takes https only). Captions are optional: the folder name describes a photo without one. Inside a folder every photo is numbered; photos 1–8 are marked "Bot gửi trước" because the bot sends a folder 8 photos at a time in this order and offers the rest. Tiles reorder by drag and drop (keyboard too) or the ⋮ menu (Lên / Xuống / Chuyển sang thư mục… / Đặt làm ảnh bìa / Sửa chú thích / Xoá), and several can be selected to move or remove. A folder shows as a grid or as a list (the choice is remembered per browser); the list shows each item's number, thumbnail and caption, edited in place. Clicking a thumbnail opens it large (photo, playable video, a YouTube or Google Drive player, or a link card for any other link; YouTube and Drive links show as videos in the grid: the YouTube thumbnail or a Drive placeholder with a play mark, `features/lodging/lib/video-link.ts`) with ‹ › and the arrow keys to step through the folder and its caption editable there. The cover (`galleryCover`) is the chosen photo, or the first photo of the first folder that has one (contracts `galleryCoverUrl`; the lists and the bot use it). The open folder is in the URL (`?folder=<id>`). Saving sends the whole `gallery` and `galleryCover` in one `PATCH`. A `PATCH` that sends a new `gallery` without `galleryCover` resets a stored cover that is no longer one of its photos to automatic (`''`); a `galleryCover` that is sent must be a photo of the resulting gallery, else 400 `VALIDATION_FAILED` (issue path `galleryCover`). Removing an item never deletes the stored file. A unit of a rooms property has its own gallery and cover with the same rules (T-193), edited on the unit page and saved with `PUT units`. Migration 0066 put every existing flat gallery into one folder "Ảnh chung". The website widget still shows only the first picture of a bot message and no video.

Bookings carry `packageIds` (distinct, in unit order) and `packageNames` (the packages' current names; `quote.packages[].name` keeps the name at quote time); calendar segments carry `packageNames`; the calendar's nightly prices are the full package's.

Booking events go to the outbox: `lodging.booking.created.v1` and `lodging.booking.{confirmed,cancelled,expired,checked_in,checked_out}.v1`. They carry ids, codes, dates, status and source, never the guest's name or phone.

## What the bot does (parts 1 and 3)

**Catalog (part 1).** Hotel bots read the properties they sell from the lodging module, through `LODGING_CATALOG_FACADE` (the same shape as the commerce catalog). A property appears only when it is active and every weekday is priced. The bot sees sell prices only (weekday rates, upcoming date-range prices, surcharges, child age bands, room prices of a rooms property), the policies, amenities, layout, its media folders (`accommodation.albums`, with album and video ids for `send_message`) and their photos flattened (`gallery`). Since T-193 each unit also carries its category, view, area, description, amenities, maximum and packages, and its own folders are listed in `albums` after the property's (ids from folder index 20 on, so a unit never shifts a property album id; label "product – unit – folder"). A target with packages lists `packages {name, bedrooms, standardAdults, standardChildren, maxGuests, weekdaySell[7]}` (Monday first, null = not sold that weekday), fewest bedrooms first; `rates` are the full package's prices, the product's `priceMin`/`priceMax` span every weekday price of the property, its rooms and all their packages (so "from" is the cheapest package), and a date-range price of a package carries its `packageName`. It never sees a cost, the internal property note, night notes or booking tags.

**Calendar, quotes and holds (part 3, T-161, [ADR-019](adr/ADR-019-bot-lodging-holds.md)).** The bot reads the calendar through `LODGING_BOT_FACADE` and never computes dates, weekdays, totals or availability itself (prompt `sales-agent@18`, `@20` since T-192 with the media-folder rules, `@21` since T-193 with the unit and package rules; tools in [the AI engine doc](06-ai-engine.md) §3.5 "Lodging holds"):

1. **Check.** For any dated question the bot calls `check_availability` with check-in, check-out, adults, children's ages and optionally a property code; it asks for whatever is missing first. The server answers the free options of the properties the bot sells (at most 5, fitting parties first, then cheapest) with nightly sell prices, Vietnamese weekday labels, surcharges and the total. When the asked place is full it says so (`requestedFull`) and the bot offers the other options. A place whose date-range price needs a longer stay comes back with its `minNights`. Past dates, stays over 60 nights and invalid parties come back as problems for the bot to explain. An option above the property's maximum party is not `fits` and never `overStandard`, so the bot does not offer it. An option names the `package` the quote picked (null when it picked packages for several targets) and its `alternatives` with their totals (null = not sold one of those nights).
2. **Prepare.** When the customer wants to keep a place, the bot asks for a name and phone and calls `prepare_hold`. Nothing is written to the calendar yet: the server checks everything again, stores one draft per conversation (30 minutes, a new prepare replaces it), and sends its own summary (place, dates with weekdays, nights, party, package, total, guest, hold time) with the quick replies **✅ Giữ chỗ** and **✏️ Sửa thông tin**. The customer may choose another package than the automatic one (`packages: [{room?, package}]`, names matched ignoring case and accents); an unknown name answers `unknown_package` with the valid names, a package not sold one of the nights `package_unavailable`. A stay priced without any package ignores `packages`. The draft stores the package ids, and the confirm books exactly those (a package deleted meanwhile answers `price_changed`).
3. **Confirm.** When the customer answers after the summary, `confirm_hold` re-quotes and creates a booking with status **Giữ chỗ**, source **Bot**, the contact link, the conversation link (messaging channels only, not the test chat) and the business's hold time, through the same booking path as staff. The bot gives the code and the expiry ("đã giữ chỗ …"); it never says the stay is booked or confirmed. If the nights were taken meanwhile, the price changed or the draft expired, the bot apologises and offers alternatives (none when the check-in day has already passed). A retried confirm returns the same booking while it is still on hold. A draft and its confirmed copy are deleted once their 30 minutes are over.
4. **Staff review.** A bot hold waits for staff until someone acts on it (any status action or edit). The Booking page marks bot bookings **Bot tạo** (amber while waiting), shows the waiting count on the **Giữ chỗ** tab and next to **Booking** in the sidebar (refreshed every minute and after every booking change), and filters by **Nguồn → Bot**. The booking sheet of a waiting bot hold says "Bot đã giữ chỗ theo yêu cầu khách — gọi xác nhận rồi bấm Xác nhận" next to **Mở hội thoại**: read the chat, call the guest, then **Xác nhận**, **Gia hạn giữ** or **Huỷ**. An untouched hold expires like any other.

`prepare_hold`, `confirm_hold` and `cancel_hold_draft` need the bot's **Chốt đơn** switch; without it the bot only checks availability. Hotel bots have no retail order tools.

Holds placed from the bot's test chat (playground) are real holds: they block the nights and count in the waiting badge. Cancel them in **Booking** after testing.
