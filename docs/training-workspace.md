# Bot training workspace

The bot workspace follows the Preny layout (T-047): a training menu (home, products, instructions, information/FAQ, integrations, image library, a Settings group with bot setup, quick replies and edit history, and a Development group with the Facebook ads tool) next to the section content, and the test chat docked on the right with up to three FAQ questions as suggestions. Below desktop widths the test chat opens in a sheet.

The home page is a four-step checklist in this order: connect a platform, products, situation instructions, FAQs. A newly created bot opens on its integrations page, because a bot without a channel cannot answer anyone.

Products reuse the workspace's operational commerce catalog, including its `visibleToBot` flag and optional `botInstructions` guidance (up to 4000 characters) and up to 5 `imageUrls` (https links, 2000 characters each; migration `0014_product_images.sql`, column `products.image_urls jsonb not null default '[]'` with a CHECK that it is an array of at most 5) that the bot may send when a customer asks to see the product (see `06-ai-engine.md`, bot-sent pictures; edited in the product form under "Ảnh sản phẩm"). They are not copied into a separate bot catalog. Integrations list the pages bound to the bot; connecting a page from there binds it to the bot (see `meta-inbox.md`). FAQ and settings retain their existing APIs.

## Training materials

The knowledge module owns `training_items` and `training_history` (migration `0008_training.sql`). The bot module exposes the HTTP routes and verifies bot ownership before calling the knowledge facade. Every route requires authentication and workspace membership. Queries use a workspace transaction and forced row level security. Material writes and history insertion commit together.

- `GET /api/v1/bots/{botId}/training-items`: up to 500 materials, newest updates first.
- `POST /api/v1/bots/{botId}/training-items`: create a material.
- `POST /api/v1/bots/{botId}/training-items/starter-rules`: add the starter rules the bot does not have yet (201, `{data: TrainingItemDto[]}` with only the created items; an empty list when nothing was missing).
- `PATCH /api/v1/bots/{botId}/training-items/{itemId}`: edit a material without changing its kind.
- `DELETE /api/v1/bots/{botId}/training-items/{itemId}`: delete a material; preserve its history.
- `GET /api/v1/bots/{botId}/training-history`: latest 200 changes (materials, FAQs, settings).
- `POST /api/v1/bots/{botId}/training-history/{historyId}/restore`: put back the state before a restorable entry (204). The restore is recorded as a new entry.

A material has `kind` (`scenario`, `quick_reply`, `media`, `media_folder`), `title` (1–2000 characters), `content` (1–8000), `trigger` (0–500), nullable `mediaUrl` (HTTPS only, up to 2000 characters) and nullable `folderId` (migration `0012`). Only `media` may have a `folderId`, and it must be a `media_folder` of the same bot; deleting a folder deletes and records its media first. Boundary validation trims text and rejects empty updates.

### Instruction rules v2 (T-070)

Scenario items are the owner's instruction rules and carry two more fields (migration `0016_training_rules.sql`, additive, with defaults): `enabled` (boolean, default `true`) and `section` (`general` | `after_order`, default `general`, CHECK constraint `training_items_section_check`). A disabled rule stays saved and editable but is never given to the model, and its text no longer vouches for figures in replies. Both fields are part of the history `changes` (`enabled`, `section`; the history page labels them and shows `section` as "Quy tắc chung" / "Sau khi chốt đơn") and of the snapshot, so a toggle or a move shows in the history and can be restored. They are only meaningful for scenarios; other kinds keep the defaults.

- `general` rules always apply. They are rendered under a `## General rules` header inside `<shop_situations>`.
- `after_order` rules are rendered under a separate header that tells the model they apply only right after an order or booking was just completed or confirmed (for example when an action call succeeded). A header is omitted when its section has no enabled rule.
- At most 20 enabled rules reach the model (disabled rules do not count), the oldest first by creation time. The block is capped at 20,000 characters by cutting each rule to a common length (short rules stay whole); headers are never cut and no section is dropped. The instructions page shows an "n/20 đang bật" counter once 15 or more rules are enabled.

**Starter rules.** A new bot is seeded with a Vietnamese starter pack (`knowledge/domain/starter-rules.ts`, product copy, all editable): polite "dạ/ạ" register; address customers "anh/chị" and refer to itself "em" (use "bạn"/"mình" when the gender is unknown); quote round-thousand prices with a "k" suffix; replies of at most 40 words; ask for phone number and delivery address when a customer shows interest, unless already known; never tell the customer to wait, except when handing the conversation to staff; a payment rule that only answers from the shop information in the data and otherwise says it does not know yet and asks for a phone number so staff can help; and one `after_order` rule ("Sau khi chốt đơn": thank the customer in at most 20 words). The pack states no shop policy (no cash on delivery or inspection claims) and has no language rule, because the prompt already follows the bot and customer language. It never tells the bot to create orders without the customer's confirmation or to skip asking for a name. The price rule contains no sample amount on purpose: rule text is trusted text for number grounding, so an example price would vouch for an invented one. The table has no key column, so the exact title is the identity: `POST …/training-items/starter-rules` creates only the rules whose title is missing (reading every scenario title of the bot, not the 500-item list) and records one history entry per created rule; it is idempotent and serialized per bot with a transaction-scoped advisory lock, so two concurrent calls never duplicate a rule. A rule the owner deleted or renamed comes back the next time the endpoint is called. Bot creation seeds the same pack as best effort after the bot is saved: a seeding failure is logged (without data) and never fails the creation, and the seeded rules record no history entries. Because new bots start with these rules, the "situation instructions" step of the home checklist counts as done from the start; this is intentional.

**System rules (not editable).** Independently of the owner's rules, the `sales-agent` prompt enforces: facts only from the knowledge, situation, catalog and action blocks; the numbers guardrail (every price, quantity and phone number in a reply must appear in trusted text; "99k" and "99.000đ" denote the same amount); the bot's configured pronouns; greeting only in the first reply; answering every question of the latest customer turn; handoff rules; the confirmation gate for action calls; and treating customer content as data. Owner rules cannot override them (see `06-ai-engine.md`).

History stores action, title, kind, record ID, timestamp, the actor's display name (snapshotted through the identity facade; empty for system changes), the changed fields (`changes: [{field, before, after}]`) and, for item updates and deletions, the item before the change (`snapshot`). An entry is `restorable` when it is a settings change with field changes, or an item creation/update/deletion; FAQ entries are not restorable. Restoring a creation deletes the item; restoring an update or deletion saves the snapshot back (outside any folder if the folder no longer exists). Bot ownership and workspace isolation also apply to history.

Instruction scenarios provide guidance for bot generation through the knowledge public API. Quick replies are saved team reply templates; automatic expansion in a message composer is a separate capability. The image library stores references and descriptions: it does not upload, fetch or analyze remote files. Media URLs open only after an explicit user click. The history also records successful FAQ and bot settings changes. Legacy FAQ/settings writes and their history entries use sequential module transactions; material CRUD and its history use one atomic transaction. 

Bot settings include `themeColor` (`#RRGGBB`, default `#FF6600`) and `replySchedule` (`{mode: always|custom, start, end}` in `HH:MM`, Vietnam time, `[start, end)`, `end < start` spans midnight). Outside the reply hours the Meta inbox processor stores the message without answering, so staff handle it. Bot settings also include `autoResumeAfterManual` (default true), `humanTakeoverMinutes` (1–1440, default 5), and `silenceOnExternalReply` (default true). Old JSON settings receive these defaults on rehydration; partial updates never re-apply defaults to fields the client did not send. Partial updates leave unrelated handover values unchanged. The conversation module enforces these values on staff sends and Meta echoes.

The HTTP contract lives in `packages/contracts/src/bot/bot.routes.ts`; request/response schemas live in `packages/contracts/src/knowledge/knowledge.schema.ts`. Vietnamese and English labels are isolated in the `training` i18n namespace.

## Factual knowledge and response instructions (T-085)

The bot menu names the two destinations explicitly: Business information contains factual question/answer knowledge (address, opening hours, shipping/return policies); Response instructions contains tone and behavioral rules (how to ask questions, advise and hand off). Both pages show purpose, examples and a link to the other destination. Existing knowledge, rules, priority and retrieval behavior are unchanged. Products and media retain their existing dedicated pages.

## Direct activation controls (T-084)

The bot list and bot training menu use the same named on/off switch. Users need bots.manage; pending changes disable the switch. List/detail caches update together and revert when the API rejects a save. Switching off keeps page assignments intact and uses the existing runtime stop checks. Existing basic and advanced settings remain under Bot settings.
