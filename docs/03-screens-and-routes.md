# 03 — Screen & route map

> 1-to-1 mapping against the observed Preny routes (`app.preny.ai/quan-ly/...`). Preny's per-field/column/button details: [`reference/preny-dashboard-spec.md`](reference/preny-dashboard-spec.md).
> Our convention: **English, kebab-case** routes in code (`/app/...`), Vietnamese display labels via i18n. If the business wants Vietnamese URLs like Preny, use next-intl `pathnames` to localize — without changing the structure.

## 1. App shell

- **Header:** logo → profile; Home; Guide; Buy plan; AI credit balance badge (→ AI wallet); language selector; notification bell [+]; avatar.
- **Banner** when on the free plan / plan about to expire / wallet empty / channel error.
  - Implemented (T-091): the AI-message banner on every `/app` page, from `GET /api/v1/billing/usage` — amber from 80% of the account's allowance, red once it is spent (bots hand customers to staff until the cycle renews). Not shown for a business that is not metered yet.
- **Sidebar** is collapsible; items carry badges ("Miễn phí" (Free), "Hot", unread count).
- **Two layers, plus the back-office.** The _account layer_ (`/app/overview`, `/app/businesses`, `/app/businesses/new`) shows summary statistics over every business of the signed-in user, a gallery of those businesses (`/app/businesses`: a card grid filtered by name, industry and creation time, sorted newest, oldest or by name; filters live in the URL) and lets the owner create a business; it has its own sidebar (overview, "My businesses" as one entry leading to the gallery, new business, and for platform admins a "System administration" group with every back-office page). The _business layer_ (everything else under `/app`) is one business's management area with the sidebar below; it never shows platform pages, even for a platform admin running Rapiz HQ. The _platform back-office_ (`/admin/*`) opens inside the account layer: same sidebar, with the current back-office page marked in the "System administration" group, so the overview and "My businesses" stay one click away; platform admins also reach it from the account menu. The logo always returns to the account layer; choosing a business (overview, gallery or the business selector) enters its statistics page. A new business is created with `POST /workspaces` and opened right after.
- Common patterns: empty states with an illustration + CTA; tables with column resizing, row selection, pagination + page size; date range `dd/mm/yyyy – dd/mm/yyyy` with presets; plan-gated screens ("Nâng cấp để sử dụng" (Upgrade to use)) reuse a single component `<UpgradeGate feature=... />`.

## 6. UI design

- Do not copy Preny's identity (logo, purple-pink colors, copy) — only learn from the information architecture. Build our own design system (shadcn/ui + our own tokens), with dark mode support.
- Prioritize high information density for CRM/order tables (users are salespeople operating many hours a day), keyboard shortcuts (F2/F6 like Preny, `/` to search, `J/K` to switch conversations).

## Business selector and business information (T-144)

- The sidebar business selector lists the caller's businesses and always ends with **+ Tạo business**. It opens a dialog with a business name and a template (`Industry`, with a one-line description each; no default). Submitting calls `POST /workspaces` (the server picks the caller's only owned account, ADR-012), reloads the memberships and switches to the new business, landing on `/app/analytics`. The dialog is composed in `routes/app.tsx` and passed through `AppShell` (shared components may not import features).
- `/app/settings/profile` is one card: logo, a single editable business name, the template as a read-only summary (chosen at creation, never changed afterwards), and an optional company name in a collapsed section. Facts the AI uses (address, policies…) are entered per bot under Business information. The workspace fields `businessServices` and `productDescription` are deprecated: no longer shown, still accepted by the API.
- After a rename the session memberships (selector), the current-business query and the profile query are refreshed without a reload.
- The settings tab strips (`/app/settings`, `/app/crm-settings`, `/app/order-settings`) share `components/settings-tabs.tsx`: horizontal scroll only, baseline drawn as an inset shadow so the strip never scrolls vertically.

## SaaS routes (ADR-020, ADR-021)

- A SaaS business has no pages of its own: subscriptions live on the customer sheet (**Subscription** card, with a **History** toggle) and in the customer list's summary strip and columns. Workflow and API: [SaaS businesses](saas.md).
- `/app/crm-settings/saas-plans` (tab **Gói dịch vụ**, shown only for a SaaS business): the plan catalog — name, a price per billing period, archive / restore.
- A customer or subscription synced from a Rapiz account (Rapiz HQ) carries a **Rapiz** badge; its phone and email, and the subscription's plan, stage, period, MRR and dates, are read-only with a lock hint.

## Hotel routes (T-160)

- **Lịch căn** (`/app/calendar/$propertyId?view=month|quarter|year|list&from=&unit=`): one property's calendar, opened from the property name in a Lịch row or a phone month card; the search is the source of truth so links and Back restore the view. Lịch itself keeps its window, filters, expanded rooms and "Tìm trống" search in its URL (`/app/calendar?from&nights&area&free&open&ci&co&adults&ages`), so "← Lịch" and Back return to the same grid. Details: [lodging](lodging.md) (Calendar).
- A hotel business's sidebar shows **Lịch** (`/app/calendar`), **Booking** (`/app/bookings`) and **Căn hộ** (`/app/properties`, detail `/app/properties/$propertyId`) instead of Sản phẩm. Each page renders only for a hotel business (`HotelOnly`).
- Redirects: `/app/accommodations` → `/app/properties`, `/app/stays` → `/app/bookings` (T-155 addresses); `/app/products` and `/app/products/{id}` → `/app/properties` for a hotel business.
- The Inbox sales panel of a hotel business opens the booking sheet with the contact prefilled and lists the conversation's bookings. Workflow and API: [lodging](lodging.md).
- Bot holds (T-161, [ADR-019](adr/ADR-019-bot-lodging-holds.md)): the sidebar **Booking** item shows how many bot holds wait for staff (`GET /lodging/bookings/review-count`, polled every minute; composed in `routes/app.tsx` and passed through `AppShell` as `navCounts`). On `/app/bookings` the **Giữ chỗ** tab carries the same count, bot bookings carry a **Bot tạo** badge (amber while waiting), and the **Nguồn** filter sends `?source=`. The booking sheet of a bot booking opens with a banner: while the hold waits, "Bot đã giữ chỗ theo yêu cầu khách — gọi xác nhận rồi bấm Xác nhận"; afterwards a quiet "made by the bot" line; both with **Mở hội thoại** (`/app/inbox?conversation=`).

## Owner assistant bubble (T-170)

- Every `/app` page shows a round charcoal launcher with the Rapiz R mark (violet AI dot) bottom-right for an active **owner** of the account; billing members and business staff never see it. It is composed in `routes/app.tsx`. On bot pages (`/app/bots/{botId}/…`) it sits higher so it does not cover the **Chat thử** button.
- The launcher opens a side panel ("Thỏ Rapiz"): the owner's threads (searchable, pinned first; pinning and archiving exist in the API only), the conversation, and **Xem các bước** under an answer (tool names, ok/error, duration). While a turn runs the panel shows the steps so far. Switching business starts a fresh conversation.
- **Two modes, one conversation.** The panel's ⤢ button (desktop) expands it to the full page `/app/assistant`, also reachable from the sidebar item **Trợ lý** (owners only, after **Thống kê**). The page lists the threads on the left (a drawer on phones and tablets, ☰), shows **Trò chuyện · Lịch báo cáo · Kỹ năng · Bộ nhớ · Cài đặt** as tabs and keeps the chat at a reading width; the launcher is hidden there. ⤡ collapses it: back to the last other dashboard page, with the same thread in the side sheet. An answer that navigates from the full page also continues in the side sheet next to the page it opens, so a fill or spotlight that follows is seen. Both modes are one `AssistantPanel` instance (the page only registers a slot the panel renders into), so the thread, the text being typed, attachments and a running turn survive the switch. Anyone but an owner sees a short "owners only" note on `/app/assistant`.
- The assistant can move the owner to another `/app` page and highlight a control on the current page. Controls it may point at carry `data-assistant-target`: `products.new`, `orders.new`, `bots.new`, `channels.connect`, `products.save`, and since T-175 `bot-profile.save`, `bot-personality.save`, `business.create`, `business-settings.save`, `faq.new`, `scenario.new`, `scenario.starter` (the "Gợi ý theo ngành" button) and `quick-reply.new`.
- The launcher must not cover a page's bottom-right control. Padded pages get room after their content automatically; a bottom-right control on a full-bleed page or in a sticky bar carries `data-assistant-avoid` (keep it on a short element: the launcher moves just above it). The owner can also drag the launcher anywhere (mouse or touch; a press that moves under 5 px is still a click) or move it with Alt + arrow keys; it stays 8 px inside the viewport, is clamped again on resize, and its place is remembered per browser (`rapiz.assistant.launcherPosition`). A moved launcher stays where it was put and no longer steps over marked controls.
- The assistant can fill a form on screen that the page registered (`useAssistantForm` in `apps/web/src/lib/assistant-context.ts`); it never submits it. Registered forms (T-175 added all but `product`; spec [shop autofill](superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-owner-assistant-shop-autofill-design.md) §4):
  - `product` on `/app/products/new`: code, name, category, description, first variant's SKU and price.
  - `bot-profile` on `/app/bots/{botId}/knowledge`: the industry's profile fields except the Google Maps link, plus notes.
  - `faq` on the knowledge page: question and answer of a new FAQ.
  - `bot-personality` on `/app/bots/{botId}/instructions`: mission, greeting, pronouns, reply length, tone, emoji.
  - `scenario` on the instructions page: title, trigger, instruction and section of a new scenario.
  - `quick-reply` on `/app/bots/{botId}/quick-replies`: shortcut and content of a new quick reply (never the image).
  - `bot` on `/app/bots`: the new bot's name (only with `bots.manage` and a supported industry).
  - `business` on `/app/businesses/new`: name and industry (the page's own industry list; never the account picker).
  - `business-settings` on `/app/settings/profile`: display name and company name (never the icon).

  Editing an existing row never registers a form. The FAQ, scenario, quick-reply and new-bot forms live in dialogs: while the dialog is closed the list registers the form, and a fill opens the add dialog with the draft; while it is open the dialog registers the same id itself (the registry keeps a stack per id, so closing the dialog hands the id back to the list). After a fill that opens a dialog the panel closes and spotlights nothing; after any other fill with a save button the panel steps aside and spotlights it (for example **Lưu sản phẩm**) so the owner reviews and saves. If the form is not on the page, a toast says so.
- **Nhờ trợ lý điền mẫu** (owner only): the empty FAQ, scenario and quick-reply lists, and the business-profile card while a required basic field is empty, show this button. It sends a fixed request to the assistant (the bubble opens and sends it, or puts it in the composer while a turn runs); the knowledge, instructions, quick-reply, create-business and business-settings pages describe themselves to the assistant (title, bot id and industry).
- A change the assistant prepares (a write) shows as a card under its answer: summary, business, **Xem chi tiết** (the preview as JSON) and **Áp dụng** / **Bỏ**. Nothing changes until the owner presses **Áp dụng**. A decided card becomes one status line (applied, failed with its code, dismissed, expired), and the decision's event message appears in the thread.
- **Trợ lý nhớ gì** (brain button in the panel header) lists what the assistant remembers: about the owner, general notes and one store per business, each with its character use against the limit. The owner can forget an entry (with an inline confirm).
- Backend and flow: [04 §5](04-system-architecture.md), [ADR-024](adr/ADR-024-owner-assistant.md).

## Today screen (Business Brain v1)

**Hôm nay** (`/app/today`, BRN-05): the screen an owner opens first each morning, listing what needs doing today across every conversation of the business and why. Spec: [Business Brain v1 and Today](superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-business-brain-today-design.md); decisions: [ADR-030](adr/ADR-030-business-brain-signals.md).

- **Sidebar:** first item of the business sidebar, with a count badge (`NavCounts`) from `GET /today/counts` (counts only; polled every 3 minutes and on window focus; the full `GET /today` is read on the page only). Every member sees it; what they see depends on their role (below).
- **Header:** title and today's date, the count of each queue, **Làm mới (còn N lượt)** (`POST /today/refresh`: evaluates up to 20 conversations with unevaluated customer messages, highest priority first, within the remaining cap; it starts no evaluation after about 45 s and then says how many are left for the next click). When the daily cap is used up, a banner reads "Đã hết lượt đánh giá hôm nay — các mục chỉ dựa trên dữ liệu có sẵn" and the queues keep working from facts and older signals. In manual mode ("Chỉ khi tôi bấm") a hint explains that conversations are evaluated only on click.
- **"Rapiz đã mang lại" strip** (above the queues; owner, admin and analyst only: `analytics.view` + `contacts.view_all`): "30 ngày qua Rapiz đã mang lại N đơn · X triệu" with a range toggle **Hôm nay / 7 ngày / 30 ngày** (default 30 days), the split **AI tự chốt** vs **AI + nhân viên**, and the follow-ups sent with the replies received (`GET /brain/impact?range=today|7d|30d`, ADR-031). While the range has no attributed order and no follow-up, a one-line explainer replaces the figures.
- **"AI đã làm"** (collapsed section, shown only when not empty): today's follow-ups the care agent sent at level *làm rồi báo*, each with the customer, channel, template, text, send time and whether the customer replied (`GET /today/ai-done`).
- **Chờ duyệt** (`approval`, ADR-031) comes **first** (in the counts row too), full width: the follow-ups the care agent drafted at level *đề xuất*. Each draft shows the customer, the channel, the template (Khách im lặng / Sắp chốt / Sau mua), the draft text and the time left before the 24-hour window closes ("còn 3 giờ", amber under 2 hours). Actions: **Gửi** (`POST /today/approval/{id}/approve`), **Sửa rồi gửi** (inline editor, at most 600 characters; sends `{text}`), **Bỏ** (`POST /today/approval/{id}/reject`); the header's **Gửi tất cả** asks for a confirm, then sends the drafts on screen (`POST /today/approval/approve-all` with their ids) and says how many could not be sent. A draft leaves the list at once on any action (put back if the request fails; a 409 says it was already handled or expired), and a draft whose window has closed disappears by itself (re-checked every 30 seconds). The write actions need `contacts.edit`; other roles read the drafts only.
- **Other queues,** in this order; each is a card with a count, up to 20 items and its own empty state. A conversation appears only in the first queue it matches:

  | Queue | Key | Shows |
  |---|---|---|
  | Phàn nàn / cần người | `attention` | signal mood `complaint` or needs a person, and no staff reply since the evaluation ran |
  | Chưa trả lời | `unanswered` | the customer has waited more than 15 minutes (a fact, no evaluation needed) |
  | Sắp chốt | `ready` | stage `ready` (ready to buy or book), judged within the last 7 days, and no order or booking since |
  | Hỏi giá rồi im | `price_silent` | stage `price_asked` and the customer silent for between 24 hours and 14 days |
  | Đến hạn chăm lại | `follow_up` | the signal's follow-up time has passed, or the conversation's own follow-up date is today or earlier |
  | Việc của tôi | `tasks` | open tasks due by the end of today or overdue, assigned to me or unassigned (every role; the whole team's list stays on `GET /tasks?scope=all`) |

- **Item:** avatar or initials, customer name, channel icon, one-line reason (intent, the reason a person is needed, or the follow-up reason), relative time, stage badge and assignee. Stage labels are generic; the dashboard group (shop/hotel/saas/service) only changes the wording.
- **Item actions:** **Mở hội thoại** (`/app/inbox?conversation=<id>`) · **Soạn trả lời** (`POST /conversations/{id}/reply-draft`, one LLM call on click that counts toward the cap; the draft is kept in memory only (per business and conversation, cleared on logout, never in the URL) and `/app/inbox?conversation=<id>` opens; the composer takes it once it actually holds the text; never sent automatically) · **Thêm việc** (task dialog prefilled with the conversation and customer) · **Bỏ qua** (`POST /today/items/{conversationId}/dismiss`; hidden for the whole business until a newer customer message; the toast offers **Hoàn tác**, `DELETE` on the same path) · **Đánh giá lại** (`POST /conversations/{id}/signals/evaluate`; answers `capped` when the cap is used up) · stage menu (`PATCH /conversations/{id}/signals`; the chosen stage is locked against later evaluations).
- **Tasks section:** each task with a checkbox to complete (`POST /tasks/{id}/complete`), the due date and the assignee.
- **Role visibility:** owner and admin see the whole business. Members without `contacts.view_all` (sales, CSKH) see only conversations of customers assigned to them or unassigned, and only their own and unassigned tasks. The customer actions (Soạn trả lời, Thêm việc, Bỏ qua, Đánh giá lại, the stage menu) and Làm mới need `contacts.edit`; read-only roles (analyst) see the queues with **Mở hội thoại** only, and can still tick off their own tasks.
- **Phone:** one column; item actions move into an overflow menu.
- **Care settings:** the same page holds a second card, **Chăm sóc tự động** (ADR-031): today's usage ("Hôm nay AI đã soạn N/100 tin chăm sóc"), the Meta 24-hour rule in plain words, the level of `care.send_message` (**Đề xuất** (default) / **Làm rồi báo** / **Tự làm**, each with a one-line explanation; `GET/PUT /brain/autonomy`), the templates (Khách im lặng and Sắp chốt: on/off plus an optional instruction of up to 500 characters with an example placeholder; Sau mua: on/off plus the delay in days, 1–30; `GET /care/settings`, `PUT /care/templates/{key}`, only the changed ones are saved), and read-only facts: quiet hours 21:00–08:00 (Vietnam time), at most 2 messages per customer in 24 hours, and the daily cap. Owners and admins (`settings.manage`) change it; others read it.
- **Inbox:** a care agent message (`source: 'care'`) carries the label **Chăm sóc tự động** instead of "Bot". When the server allows `HUMAN_AGENT` replies (`humanAgentReply` on `GET /conversations/{id}`: Messenger/Instagram with `META_HUMAN_AGENT_TAG=true`), a member's composer stays open from 24 hours to 7 days after the customer's last message with the hint "Gửi với tư cách nhân viên (trong 7 ngày)"; otherwise the closed-window message stays.
- **Elsewhere:** the inbox customer panel shows the conversation's signal summary with **Đánh giá lại** and its tasks; Settings → **AI & chăm sóc** (`/app/settings/brain`, owner and admin) shows "Đã dùng N/200 lượt đánh giá hôm nay", the mode switch (Tự động khi hội thoại lắng / Chỉ khi tôi bấm), the idle wait (30–120 minutes) and the morning briefing hour (`GET/PUT /brain/settings`, `GET /brain/usage`).
- **Notifications** leading here: the morning briefing (`today.briefing`, counts only, skipped when all are zero) and task reminders (`task.due`); the complaint alert (`brain.complaint`) opens the conversation.
