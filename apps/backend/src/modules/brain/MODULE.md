# brain

**Purpose:** Business Brain v1 (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-business-brain-today-design.md`, ADR-030): one AI judgement per conversation (stage, intent, mood, needs-a-person, follow-up, rolling note), evaluated when a conversation goes idle or on request, within a daily cap; the complaint alert.

**Owned tables:** `conversation_signals` (+ `opt_out`, 0074), `signal_jobs`, `signal_runs`, `signal_usage`, `brain_settings`, `autonomy_settings` (0074, ADR-031), `brain_checkpoints` (global, app_system only: the outbox position, row `brain.signals`) (schema `packages/db/src/schema/brain.ts`, migrations 0072, 0074).

**Public API (`api/`):**
- `BRAIN_FACADE` → `signalsFor(ws, convIds)`, `evaluateNow(ws, convId, 'manual'|'refresh')` → `{status: ok|capped|nothing_new|disabled, signal}`, `usage(ws)` → `{day, used, limit}`, `settings(ws)`, `levelFor(ws, action)` (autonomy registry, `domain/autonomy.ts`: `care.send_message` default `suggest`, levels suggest · act_and_report · auto).
- `BRAIN_TODAY_REGISTRY.registerCare(source)` — the care module adds the `approval` queue ("Chờ duyệt"), due after-sales check-ins (`follow_up`, `careTemplate: after_sale`) and the weekly impact; Today and the briefing work without it (a failing source reads as empty).
- `BRAIN_RUNNER.tick()` — worker, every 15 s: outbox pass → reset capped jobs whose day came (due 5 min later, behind the day's fresh jobs) → claim 5 due jobs at a time (`FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, 2-minute lease) and evaluate them, batch after batch while jobs are due and less than 12 s passed.
- `BRAIN_RUNNER.tick()` also runs the morning briefing pass at most once a minute.
- `BRAIN_EXPORT` — `conversation_signals.csv` of the workspace data export (SET-10), keyed by `conversation_id`.
- HTTP (`packages/contracts/src/brain/`): `GET|PATCH /conversations/{id}/signals`, `POST /conversations/{id}/signals/evaluate`, `GET|PUT /brain/settings`, `GET|PUT /brain/autonomy` (PUT: `settings.manage`), `GET /brain/usage`, `GET /today`, `GET /today/counts`, `POST /today/refresh`, `POST|DELETE /today/items/{conversationId}/dismiss`, `POST /conversations/{id}/reply-draft`.

**Today (spec §5, `application/today.query.ts`, rules in `domain/today.ts`):** candidates = conversation ids flagged by a signal (`todaySignalRefs`, ids only, ≤ 5 000: complaint or needs a person, `ready` judged within 7 days, `price_asked` whose covered messages are within 14 days, follow-up due) + `ConversationFacade.todayCandidates` (waiting ≥ 15 min, reminder due by the end of the local day); without `contacts.view_all` the viewer filter (assigned to me or nobody) runs in SQL before the 1 000 cap, and the last staff reply is read only for the attention candidates. Queues in order, first match only: `attention` (complaint or needs a person, open, no staff message after `evaluated_at`), `unanswered` (open, waiting ≥ 15 min), `ready` (judged — `evaluated_at`, else the member's change — within 7 days; not when the conversation got an order — `CommerceFacade.latestOrderTimes`, orders linked to the conversation — or a stay booking — `LodgingFacade.latestBookingTimes` — created after that), `price_silent` (`price_asked`, last customer message between 24 h and 14 days ago), `follow_up` (signal follow-up ≤ now or conversation reminder before the local midnight), `tasks` (`CRM_FACADE` open tasks due before the local midnight, mine + unassigned; without `contacts.view_all`, unassigned tasks of customers the viewer may not see are left out). Spam is never listed. ≤ 20 items per queue, `count` is the full number. `GET /today/counts` (the sidebar badge, `contacts.view`) returns the counts only (no names, task list, usage or settings). Dismiss (`contacts.edit`: it hides the item for the whole business) writes only `dismissed_until_message_after = now` (a blank signal row when none exists); the item is hidden while `lastInboundAt ≤` it; `DELETE` (undo) clears it. Refresh: Today's conversations in queue order, then those with a pending idle job, whose last customer message is after `evaluated_until` (`newAfter`); ≤ 20 per call, 3 at a time, stops at the cap, and starts no evaluation after 45 s (`REFRESH_BUDGET_MS`), answering `partial` with the number `remaining`.

**Reply draft (`application/reply-draft.service.ts`):** one tier-`basic` call (prompt `brain-reply-draft@1`, 600 tokens, 20 s) from the signal note, the last 20 messages and the persona of the conversation's bot (pronouns, tone, length, emoji, language; no free text of the bot). Reserves one evaluation of the day (released when the call fails), records a `signal_runs` row (trigger `draft`); never sends. Messages are fenced as data (`fenceSafe` breaks up `<<<`/`>>>` typed by a customer). `RATE_LIMITED` when the cap is used up.

**Morning briefing (`application/briefing.ts`):** counts include `approval` (care drafts); on a local Monday the body adds the 7-day "Rapiz đã mang lại" line (care source), which alone is reason to send. Once a minute from the tick; for each active business (`WorkspaceFacade.listActiveIds`) with the briefing on, the local hour equal to `briefing_hour` (default 8) and `last_briefing_day` older than today, each active owner/admin gets `today.briefing` (in-app + push, `/app/today`, title from `notification` i18n `today.briefing.*` with the locale's plural forms, non-zero counts only, dedup key per business and day) unless all their counts are zero. A failing recipient does not stop the others; `claimBriefing` takes the day only after every recipient was handled, so a failure is retried on the next tick of the hour (the dedup key keeps the others from a second copy). No model call.
- Events published: `brain.signals.updated.v1` (outbox, same transaction as `saveEvaluation` / `saveStage`; payload conversation id, stage, optOut, version).

**Events consumed:** `conversation.message.received.v1` (outbox, transaction horizon like analytics; a fresh install starts at the current horizon). Only customer messages (not `echo`) schedule.

**Uses:** `conversation` (`signalContext`, `todayCandidates`, `get`), `crm` (`getContact`, `countOpenTasks`, `listOpenTasks`), `commerce` (`latestOrderTimes`), `lodging` (`latestBookingTimes`), `ai` (`AI_LLM_GATEWAY`, `conversationSummary`), `bot` (`getBot`, persona of a reply draft), `billing` (`entitlements` → `brain.evaluations.daily`), `notification`, `workspace` (`accountOf`, members, `listActiveIds`), `account` (`listForUser`, ownership), `identity` (`displayName` of assignees).

**Invariants:**
- An LLM call only on idle (no customer message for `idle_minutes`, default 45, 30–120), manual or refresh triggers; never per turn, no keyword rules. Tier `basic`, 600 output tokens, 20 s, one retry on invalid output; prompt `packages/ai-core/prompts/brain-signals@2.md` (v2 adds `optOut`: the customer asked not to be contacted; an answer without it reads as false).
- A customer message upserts the job: `run_after = max(existing, at + idle)`. Skips (job dropped, no call): manual mode, test conversation, not open (resolved/spam), no customer message after `evaluated_until`, newest customer message older than a day (`stale`, a job that waited out a cap). An idle job of a business whose day is used up is capped before the conversation is read.
- Cap per business per day (Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh): reserved atomically before the call (`… on conflict do update … where count < limit`), released when the call fails (error/timeout, not an invalid answer). Plan without the key → 200 (read through `BillingFacade.entitlements`, ADR-030); `null` → unlimited. Capped job waits until the next local midnight (+ 5 min), then runs again; its attempts restart.
- Each model call writes one `signal_runs` row. Invalid twice → outcome `invalid`, signals unchanged. Invalid means unparsable JSON or an unknown stage/mood/boolean; over-long text is cut to the column limits and an unusable `followUpAt` (no time, past, > 60 days) becomes none (a time without an offset is read as Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh).
- Write rules (`saveEvaluation`, applied to the STORED row in one upsert): only the model's columns are written; `stage_locked` keeps the stored stage; `evaluated_until` = channel time of the last covered message, never moved back (`greatest`), so covered messages are never paid for again (accepted limit: a webhook stored late behind an already covered message is not sent to the model); `dismissed_until_message_after` cleared only when a newer customer message was covered. A member's stage change (`saveStage`) and dismiss (`dismissSignal`) write only their own columns. Every write bumps `version`.
- `PATCH …/signals` with a `stage` always locks it; `{stage, stageLocked: false}` is refused (400).
- Complaint alert only on the transition to `mood = complaint`: account owner(s) + assignee, `dedupKey brain.complaint:<conv>:<day>`, in-app + push.

**Do not:** read other modules' tables (the conversation's messages come through `signalContext`); send names, phones or addresses to the model in the fact line; move CRM status from signals (phase 2).
