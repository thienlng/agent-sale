# commerce

**Purpose:** the shop's catalog and orders — warehouses, products with variants, inventory with a movement log, orders with items, status flow and history, order settings, and the sales overview (docs/02 ORD-01..05, ORD-11).

**Lodging (T-155):** hotel workspaces add whole-property metadata on `products` and commerce-owned `stay_requests` (migration 0035). See [lodging operations](../../../../../docs/lodging.md) and ADR-013 for date quotes, explicit staff confirmation, overlap exclusion, idempotency, permissions and API pagination. Lodging never enters retail stock/order lines.

**Owned tables:** `warehouses`, `products`, `product_variants`, `inventory`, `inventory_movements`, `order_counters`, `orders`, `order_items`, `order_status_history`, `order_settings`, `bot_products`, `stay_requests`, `bot_order_drafts` (tenant, forced RLS — migrations 0004, 0005, 0014, 0029, 0035, 0039, 0040 and 0050; schema in `packages/db/src/schema/commerce.ts`).

**Public API (`api/`):** `COMMERCE_FACADE` → `getOrder(workspaceId, orderId)` and the bot's order flow (ADR-016, used by `ai`): `prepareBotOrder`, `confirmBotOrder`, `cancelBotOrderDraft`, `hasBotOrderDraft` (see **Bot orders** below); `CATALOG_FACADE` → `searchBotCatalog(workspaceId, bot, {query?, maxPriceVnd?, inStockOnly?, limit})` (the bot's `search_products` tool, ADR-014 — ranked by pg_trgm `word_similarity` over the folded `search_text` and category, floor 0.3, name/id tie-break, max 300 rows — `getBotProductsByIds` and `countBotCatalog`; read-only, active and `visibleToBot` only, with `imageUrls`; `bot` is `{ botId, catalogMode }` and `selected` restricts to the bot's selection, T-080); `CATALOG_SYNC_FACADE` → the PMS sync's writes (ADR-017, used by `integration`; see **PMS-linked products** below): `previewExternalMatch`, `upsertExternalProduct`, `removeExternalProducts`, `setExternalStock`, `importExternalImage` (throws `ExternalImageError` with a short `reason`), `releaseExternalSource`, `countExternalProducts`, `linkedExternalIds` (external ids of active linked products and of their live linked variants), `archiveUnlinkedVariants` (archives live variants without an external id of the source's products — the Rapiz-only variants an adopted product kept; the integration calls it after a complete full import). `ORDER_PUSH_FACADE` → the PMS order push (ADR-017, used by `integration`; see **Order push** below): `claimOrdersForPush`, `pushableOrder`, `markOrderPushed`, `markOrderPushFailed`, `requeueOrderPush`, `applyExternalOrderStatus`, `pushedOrderCount`. HTTP (workspace-scoped): `/warehouses`, `/products` (list: `source=kiotviet` = linked to KiotViet, `source=rapiz` = not linked; `ProductDto.external` = `{ source, id }` or null), `/products/{id}/stock`, `PATCH /products/{id}/bot-settings` (`catalog.manage`; `{ visibleToBot?, botInstructions? }`, at least one; any product — the way to edit a PMS-synced product whose data the full form refuses), `POST /products/images` (see below), `/product-variants`, `/bots/{botId}/products` (GET; PUT replaces the selection, `catalog.manage`; selecting a product of another workspace, or for a bot of another workspace, is 404 — the composite keys refuse the row. Commerce does not read `bots`, so an empty PUT or a GET for another workspace's bot sees no rows: 200 with nothing changed or `[]`, never another workspace's data. Saves of one bot's selection are serialized by an advisory lock; creating a product with `botId` adds one row), `/orders` (list: Excel-style column filters — `source`, `carrier` and `creator` comma lists, OR within a column and AND across columns; `creator` takes staff user ids, `bot_pending` (bot orders waiting for review, cancelled ones excluded) and `bot_approved`; accent- and case-insensitive "contains" on `code`, `tracking`, `customer` (customer or recipient name), `phone` (customer or recipient phone, digits only, `0…` and `84…` find each other), `address` (recipient address, ward, province), `product` (any line's product or variant name) and `note` (internal or printed) via `lower(unaccent(…))` against the `foldVietnamese`-folded text, no index; inclusive VND ranges `<total|cod|subtotal|shipping|gross|paid><Min|Max>`; `sort=<column>_<asc|desc>`, ties by placed time descending then code, carriers nulls last; `facets.counts.{source,creator,carrier}` count per value under every filter except that column's own, one GROUP BY each; the deprecated `createdBy` and `review=pending` join the `creator` list; mapping in `application/order-list-filter.ts`, SQL in `infrastructure/persistence/order-list.sql.ts`), `/orders/overview`, `GET /orders/review-count` (`orders.view`), `/orders/{id}`, `/orders/{id}/status` (`OrderSummaryDto.external` / `OrderDto.external` = `{ state: pending|pushed|failed, code, status, error }` or null; `OrderDto.stockState` may be `external`), `POST /orders/{id}/approve` (`orders.manage`), `/order-settings`. Contracts: `packages/contracts/src/commerce/`.

**Events consumed:** none. Uses the `identity` facade (name snapshots), the `workspace` facade (time zone), the `bot` facade (a bot's catalog mode for bot orders) and the `crm` facade (`upsertLeadByPhone` after a bot order).

**Invariants:**

- Money is integer VND; amounts follow docs/05 §6 exactly (`computeAmounts` in `packages/shared`, shared with the order form; unit-tested). `gross` ("Tổng số tiền") is before the discount, `total` ("Tiền cần thu") after it.
- Lines can be edited (`PATCH /orders/{id}` with `items`) only while the order holds its goods (`stock_state = reserved`; never once pushed, `external`); otherwise 409 and nothing changes. The reservation moves by the per-variant difference (`reservationChanges`); a variant already on the order keeps its snapshot (names, cost, price unless a new price is sent), a new variant must be on sale and takes the catalog price.
- Source field policies (`order-rules.ts`): saving an order checks its source's `create` fields; creating it in, or moving it into, a fulfilment status also checks the `confirm` fields; editing the people of an order re-checks the stage it is in. Settings saves carry `version` (stale → 409).
- Stock follows the order (`domain/order-stock.ts`): a live order holds its goods (`reserved`), the first shipped status (delivering, delivered, delivery failed, returning) takes them out, `cancelled` gives held goods back, goods already out can only come back as `returned`. An order pushed to a PMS is `external`: its stock is the PMS's and no later status moves Rapiz stock. Every change writes `inventory_movements`.
- Concurrency: edits and status changes lock the order row (`SELECT … FOR UPDATE`), stock-takes lock the inventory row; held stock is never clamped, so a negative hold fails the CHECK constraint instead of hiding a bug.
- Creating an order is idempotent per `clientRequestId` (one per form draft): a retry or double submit returns the first order.
- Overselling is allowed on purpose for staff orders (pre-orders, shops that do not track stock): they may take more than `on_hand − reserved`; the order form warns per line. Revisit if a shop needs a hard limit (owner decision). Bot orders never oversell (see below).
- Products carry up to 5 `imageUrls` (https only, 2000 characters each; migration 0014, `products.image_urls`); they are returned by the bot catalog methods (`searchBotCatalog`, `getBotProductsByIds`) so the bot can send them (the AI module builds the allowlist, never the URLs into the prompt). The AI module reads the catalog only on demand through the bot's tools (`searchBotCatalog`, `getBotProductsByIds`, `countBotCatalog`); the earlier server-side guessing (`getBotProducts`, `listBotCatalog`, `botProductSearches`) was removed.
- Search treats `%`, `_` and `\` literally.
- Order codes are `DH` + 6 digits, incrementing per workspace (`order_counters`, row-locked by the upsert).
- Optional statuses (awaiting print, printed) can only be used when switched on in order settings; statuses listed as "require note" reject a change without a note.
- Variants are archived, never deleted (past orders reference them); SKUs and product codes are unique per workspace, case-insensitive.
- Overview figures are per local day in the workspace time zone and use the same rows as the order list (sales = all but cancelled; revenue/profit/closed = `CLOSED_ORDER_STATUSES`).
- Names of who created/changed an order are snapshotted (the `users` table is global).

**Bot orders (ADR-016, `bot-order.service.ts`, `order-review.service.ts`):**

- `prepareBotOrder` resolves SKUs (case-insensitive) to variants and checks first that the bot may sell the product (visible to the bot and, for a `selected` bot, in `bot_products`; the `catalogMode` comes from the bot facade), then that the variant and product are live. It checks `on_hand − reserved` at the default warehouse and the recipient (`normalizeVnPhone`), and prices the lines from the catalog with `computeAmounts`. Problems (`unknown_sku` — also for products outside the bot's scope, whatever their status, `inactive`, `insufficient_stock` with `available`, `missing_name`, `missing_phone`, `invalid_phone`, `missing_address`) store nothing. Otherwise it upserts the conversation's single draft (30 min) and returns the server-built summary (`domain/order-summary.ts`, Vietnamese, ≤ 2 000 characters) and the product ids.
- `confirmBotOrder` locks the draft, re-prices it (a different `amounts_hash` answers `expired`), takes the order code (`nextCode`, the order counter lock, as staff create does), locks the stock rows in variant order and re-checks them (`insufficient_stock`), then in one transaction creates the order and deletes the draft. `inventory.apply` also writes its rows in variant order, so bot and staff orders cannot deadlock. The bot's catalog scope is not checked again (the customer agreed to the summary; staff review the order). The order has status `new`, the source taken from the draft's channel, `created_by_kind = 'bot'`, the bot's name, `review_state = 'pending'` and the `conversation_id` (null for a test-chat draft, channel `other`: the playground id is not an inbox conversation, so the orders list shows "Chat thử" instead of a link). It reserves stock and writes history "Bot tạo đơn từ hội thoại". It is idempotent per `bot:<conversationId>:<amounts_hash>:<draft created_at ms>`: a retry of one draft returns its order, the same basket prepared again later is a new order. A failed confirmation (expired, out of stock, price changed) throws inside the transaction, so it rolls back without using an order code; the draft is then deleted in its own short transaction (only that version). The CRM customer is then upserted by phone, best effort; the CRM takes no address.
- `POST /orders/{id}/approve`: only bot orders (otherwise 409 `NOT_BOT_ORDER`). `approve:true` is idempotent and records the reviewer, the time and history "Đã duyệt đơn do bot tạo". `approve:false` goes back to `pending` only for the same user within 5 s (otherwise 409 `UNDO_NOT_ALLOWED`). The review count and `creator=bot_pending` (alias `review=pending`) leave out cancelled orders. Staff orders are `created_by_kind = 'agent'` with no review state.
- Order summaries carry `itemsPreview` (at most 10 lines; the list cell shows 3) and `itemCount` (all lines). `itemsPreview[].imageUrl` is the product's current first picture, joined at read time in the list and the detail.

**PMS-linked products (ADR-017, `catalog-sync.service.ts`, `domain/external-lock.ts`):**

- Link columns: `products.external_source` + `external_id` (always set and cleared together, enforced by `products_external_pair_check`; unique per workspace and source) and `product_variants.external_id` (unique per workspace; the source is the product's). `releaseExternalSource` clears both on the source's products and their variants and keeps every other value, so the products become ordinary Rapiz products.
- Locked fields: on a product with a link, `PUT /products/{id}` refuses a change to `name`, `description`, `category`, `imageUrls` (order included), `status` (archiving) or `variants` (sku, name, price, cost, weight, added or removed, by id; order is free), and `POST /products/{id}/stock` refuses any stock-take: 409 `CONFLICT` with `details = { reason: 'EXTERNAL_SOURCE', field }` (`field` = the first changed one, or `stock`). `code`, `visibleToBot`, `botInstructions` and the bot selection stay editable; a kept variant keeps its `external_id`.
- `upsertExternalProduct` (one transaction, serialized per `(workspace, source, externalId)` by `pg_advisory_xact_lock`; the product row is read `FOR UPDATE`, as `updateProduct` and the bot-settings route do, so a sync and a staff save never overwrite each other): finds the product by `(source, externalId)`, else adopts the one unlinked, non-accommodation Rapiz product whose live variants carry the item's SKUs (case-insensitive), else creates one (`code` = first SKU, `-KV<externalId>` appended when taken; visible to the bot, no instructions). It writes only the linked fields. Variants match by `external_id` (an archived one comes back), then a live one by SKU; staff `cost` is kept (new variants 0); variants of the product missing from the item are archived, unless the call passes `{ partial: true }` (the item carries part of the PMS group: other live variants are kept, known variants keep their order and new ones follow); a variant id the PMS moved from another product archives and unlinks that variant. An input SKU used by a live variant of another product is skipped and returned in `skipped`. When no input variant can be kept (all skipped, or none sent) nothing is written: no product is created and a linked one stays as it was (`applied: false`, `productId` null when none exists). Name (200), description (5 000), category and variant names (120) are cut to the form's limits; codes and SKUs are stored verbatim (match keys); a new product's code is the first kept SKU. A 23505 on `products_external` / `product_variants_external` (a lost race) is `ExternalLinkConflictError` (retryable), never `DuplicateCodeError`. New variants get an empty stock row at the default warehouse (no movement: nothing moved).
- `setExternalStock`: for each live variant linked to the source, `delta = available − on_hand` at the default warehouse (row locked, variant-id order); a non-zero delta goes through `inventory.apply` (reason `adjust`, `ref_type = 'external'`, no actor), so every change has a movement and a repeat writes nothing. No default warehouse → `NoWarehouseError`.
- `removeExternalProducts` archives (never deletes) the linked products whose own external id is listed and the live variants of the source's products whose external id is listed (a PMS can delete one variant); `previewExternalMatch` counts distinct codes matching a live SKU (`matched`), the rest (`created`), and active unlinked products matching none (`rapizOnly`).
- `importExternalImage` reuses `downloadImage` (public hosts, 10 MiB, JPEG/PNG only; WebP → `unsupported_format`) through the `EXTERNAL_IMAGE_DOWNLOADER` port and stores `products/<workspaceId>/<uuidv7>.<jpg|png>` in `PRODUCT_IMAGE_STORAGE`, like an upload.
**Order push (ADR-017, spec §7, `order-push.service.ts`, `domain/order-push.ts`, `infrastructure/persistence/order-push.drizzle.ts`):**

- Columns (0040): `orders.external_source`, `external_id`, `external_code`, `external_status`, `external_state` (`pending | pushed | failed`), `external_error` (`{ code, params? }`, PII-free), `external_pushed_at`. Migration 0050 adds `external` to `orders_stock_state_check`, the unique `orders_external (workspace_id, external_source, external_id)`, the scan index `orders_push_unclaimed_idx` and `orders_push_pending_idx (workspace_id, updated_at) where external_state = 'pending'` (stale claims; all declared in `packages/db/src/schema/commerce.ts` too). `orders.update` never writes these columns (an edit cannot undo a push); only `CommerceTx.orderPush` does.
- Eligible: `external_state is null`, not deleted, not cancelled, `created_at >= since` (the connection), and either a staff order (`created_by_kind = 'agent'`) or a bot order approved more than 5 s ago (`APPROVE_UNDO_WINDOW_MS`, strict, so an approval that can still be undone is never pushed). `claimOrdersForPush` moves up to `limit` of them to `pending` with the source in one `UPDATE … WHERE id IN (SELECT … FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED)`: concurrent scans never claim one order twice.
- `pushableOrder` (row locked): null unless the order is `pending`; a pending order cancelled or deleted since its claim loses the claim (`external_state` and `external_source` back to null, not `failed`) and gets null, so reviving it queues it again; otherwise the PMS-neutral payload (notes joined ≤ 500 characters, line name "product – variant", order discount, the shipping fee the customer pays, full recipient address) or a problem with the order `code` (for the caller's log): `empty`, or `unlinked_product` with the first line whose variant has no `external_id` on a product linked to that source.
- `markOrderPushed` (order row locked) accepts `pending`, `failed` or no claim (the source then defaults to `kiotviet`); the same external id again is a no-op, another one on a pushed order throws `OrderAlreadyPushedError` (409, `reason: ALREADY_PUSHED`, the existing `externalCode`; the first refs stay), an external id another order already has throws `OrderExternalLinkConflictError` (409, `reason: EXTERNAL_ORDER_LINK_RACE`, from the unique `orders_external`). It stores the refs, `pushed`, `external_pushed_at`, clears the error; a `reserved` order gives its hold back through `inventory.apply` (one `release` movement per line, `ref_type = 'order'`); `stock_state` becomes `external` (`stockTransition('external', any)` moves nothing); history "Đã đẩy sang KiotViet (<code>)". A cancelled order is still recorded as pushed (the PMS has it) and the call answers `{ cancelled: true }` (otherwise `false`): the integration then surfaces it to staff (`order_pushed_cancelled`).
- While a push is `pending`, `PATCH /orders/{id}` refuses lines and amounts (`items`, discount, shipping fees, free shipping, surcharge): 409 `OrderItemsLockedError` with `reason: PUSH_PENDING`; notes, people, carrier and payment stay editable.
- `markOrderPushFailed` sets `failed` with the error only from `pending` (otherwise no-op); `requeueOrderPush(ws, orderId, since)` ("Thử lại") moves `failed` back to null (state and source) and answers `requeued`, otherwise 409 `CONFLICT` (`reason: PUSH_NOT_FAILED`); a failed order created before `since` (the current connection) stays failed and answers `before_since` (no scan would claim it).
- The claim stamps `updated_at`; `stalePendingOrders(ws, source, since, olderThan, limit)` lists pending orders of the source created since `since` and last updated before `olderThan` (the integration re-queues those its queue lost); `releasePendingPushes(ws, source, olderThan?)` (disconnect, push turned off; with `olderThan` the runner's sweep while push is off) moves the pending claims of the source (last updated before `olderThan` when given) back to no push state.
- `applyExternalOrderStatus` finds the order by `(external_source, external_id)` (locked), stores `external_status`, and on a PMS cancellation of a live order sets `cancelled` with history "Đơn đã huỷ trên KiotViet" (no stock moves); `unknown` when no order matches.

**Do not:** read other modules' tables; compute money with floats; update `inventory` without a movement; delete orders or variants.

**No database:** routes answer 503 `UNAVAILABLE` (the SQL search and aggregates have no faithful in-memory fake). Test with `test/commerce.integration.spec.ts`.

**Test:** `pnpm --filter @agent-sales/backend test -- commerce` · integration: `test/commerce.integration.spec.ts`, `__tests__/bot-orders.integration.spec.ts`, `__tests__/catalog-sync.integration.spec.ts`, `__tests__/order-push.integration.spec.ts`

**Product pictures (`POST /products/images`, `catalog.manage`):** body `{ mimeType: image/jpeg|png, base64 }`; the bytes are verified by magic bytes (max 1 MiB decoded; request body limit 2 MiB, raised per route via `@RouteConfig({ bodyLimit })` + the `onRoute` hook in `bootstrap.ts`), stored by `ProductImageService` in Rapiz's own S3-compatible storage under `products/<workspaceId>/<uuidv7>.<jpg|png>` and answered `201 { url }` (`${S3_PUBLIC_URL}/<key>`). **Product pictures are stored as JPEG or PNG only because Messenger rejects WebP inside multi-image albums (#-1 / 2018012);** a WebP mime or body is 400 `INVALID_IMAGE` (the web converts WebP files to JPEG before upload). Never falls back to local disk. Errors: 400 `VALIDATION_FAILED` with `details.reason = INVALID_IMAGE`; 503 `UNAVAILABLE` with `details.reason = MEDIA_STORAGE_UNAVAILABLE` (storage not configured or down). Adapter: `common/storage` (`S3PublicObjectStorage`, shared with knowledge).

**Only uploaded pictures are accepted.** On product create/update, every `imageUrls[i]` and accommodation `gallery[i].url` must be a Rapiz-stored file of the same workspace (`${S3_PUBLIC_URL}/products/<workspaceId>/<file>.<jpg|png>`, checked in `CatalogService` with `domain/own-image.ts`). Anything else (external link, another workspace's file, traversal, storage not configured) is 400 `VALIDATION_FAILED` with `details = { reason: 'IMAGE_NOT_UPLOADED', field: 'imageUrls[i]' | 'gallery[i].url' }`. An update that keeps a value already stored on the product is not exempt: legacy external URLs must be gone, so run the import CLI first in every environment.

**Import CLI for linked pictures.** `pnpm --filter @agent-sales/backend product-images:import [--dry-run] [--workspace <id>]` (needs `DATABASE_SYSTEM_URL` and the `S3_*` env) downloads every picture and gallery photo that is not yet under `${S3_PUBLIC_URL}/products/<its workspace>/` (15 s timeout, at most 3 redirects, 10 MiB, `Accept: image/jpeg,image/png;q=0.9,*/*;q=0.1`, magic-byte check; WebP or any other format fails that picture as `unsupported_format` and it keeps its old URL), stores it as `products/<workspaceId>/<uuidv7>.<ext>` and rewrites the URL in place, one transaction per product. Idempotent (failed pictures keep their old URL and are retried next run); one upload per source URL per workspace per run; prints per-workspace counts (found, imported, already stored, failed by reason) and exits 1 if anything failed. Code: `domain/image-import.ts` (pure) and `infrastructure/image-import/`.
